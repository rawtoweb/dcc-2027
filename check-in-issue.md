# Check-in issue

Body of the pinned issue "Check in" on rawtoweb/dcc-2027 (label check-in). Opened before the participant information goes out; stays open until the workshop.

---

Welcome to the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans" at the Data Curation Conference 2027 (Thursday 14 January 2027, 10:45 to 12:00, University of Basel).

This issue is your check-in, step 2 of the pre-work. Write one comment below with your first name, your institution and one line on the data you work with. That is the whole test: if your comment appears, your account works and you know where to type.

You use comments on issues three times in this workshop. In step 3 the form for your dataset opens an issue. The review of the data package built from it happens in comments on that issue. And on the day you post one comment on the issue of a published package, on what a human reader still misses. Each change that follows names the person who proposed it, so the record shows who contributed what.

Step 1 of the pre-work is the survey and step 3 is your dataset. Both are on the workshop website: https://rawtoweb.github.io/website/dcc-2027/

Questions go in a comment here as well.

Body of the pinned issue "Check in" on rawtoweb/dcc-2027 (label check-in). Opened before the participant information goes out; stays open until the workshop.

Welcome to the workshop "From raw data to a website for humans" at the Data Curation Conference 2027 (Thursday 14 January 2027, 10:45 to 12:00, University of Basel).

This issue is your check-in, step 2 of the pre-work. Write one comment below with your first name, your institution and one line on the data you work with. That is the whole test: if your comment appears, your account works and you know where to type on the day.

In the workshop a raw dataset becomes a published data package in front of the room, and every change you propose to its documentation is a comment on an issue of that package's repository. I merge the proposals live, and each commit names the person who proposed it, so the record shows who contributed what.

Step 1 of the pre-work is the survey, and the optional steps are on the workshop website: https://rawtoweb.github.io/website/dcc-2027/

Questions go in a comment here as well.
